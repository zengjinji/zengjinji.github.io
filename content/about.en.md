Hello, I'm **zengjinji**. Welcome to my personal blog.

This is where I keep notes on engineering, reading, and everyday learning. I hope each post helps me—and anyone facing a similar problem—find a clearer path the next time around.

## What you'll find here

- **Engineering practice**: implementation notes, troubleshooting, and the reasoning behind a solution.
- **Learning notes**: working through new ideas in my own words.
- **Everyday observations**: thoughts worth keeping and revisiting.

## About the site

The blog is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and published on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). Posts are stored as Markdown, and the pages do not depend on external fonts or scripts.

English is the default language. Use the language button next to About to switch between English and Chinese, including the translated version of the article you're reading.
